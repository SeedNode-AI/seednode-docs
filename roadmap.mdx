## Phases

| Phase | Timeline | Goal | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** — Reputation + Perks | 0–6 mo | Validate that people will show their wallet in exchange for real benefits | Partner with 2–3 Web3-native merchants/services: discounts, allowlists, event access |
| **Phase 2** — Uncollateralized DeFi Credit | 6–18 mo | Prove the score actually predicts repayment behavior | Integrate with 3Jane / Goldfinch-style protocols; Ring drives real on-chain credit lines |
| **Phase 3** — Real-World Institutions (vision) | 18 mo+ | Let on-chain reputation shape real-world credit terms | Requires legal structures, licensing, or partner institutions; the cover scenario lands here |

## Milestones

| Timeline | Milestone | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–4 | Architecture finalized; GitHub repos and docs scaffolded; data indexing layer connected | ✅ Shipped |
| Weeks 5–8 | Scoring engine V1 (rules-based) complete; ML anomaly detection planned for V1.1; Ring issuance logic on testnet | ✅ Shipped — Ring is live on Base Sepolia, not just testnet logic |
| Weeks 9–12 | Website live: wallet connect, reputation snapshot, share & referral growth loop | ✅ Core flow shipped (wallet connect + live scoring); growth loop in progress |
| Months 4–6 | First 2–3 Web3-native merchant partnerships live; real feedback data collected | ⏳ Not started |
| Months 6–18 | Evaluate and integrate uncollateralized DeFi lending; validate the score's predictive power | ⏳ Not started |

*As of November 2026, execution is ahead of the original 12-week schedule on the technical milestones — scoring, chain coverage, and credential issuance are live in production, not just on testnet.*

## Exploratory directions (not committed)

These are directions we're watching, not commitments on the timeline above — no scoping or build work has started.

- **Phase 2+ (exploratory, no committed timeline):** expose a public API so third-party protocols and autonomous agents can query Ring reputation data programmatically; evaluating compatibility with emerging agent-identity standards (e.g. ERC-8004) as the space matures.
